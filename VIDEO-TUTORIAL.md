# DevWorkspace Pro - Rubutaccen Jagora don Video Tutorial

Script don ɗaukar video tutorial (5-7 minti). Kowane sashe yana da: **abun da ka nuna a screen** + **abin da ka ce**.

---

## BABI 1: Fara (0:00 - 0:30)
**Screen:** Buɗe app a browser
**Ce:**
> "Sannu ku! A wannan video zan nuna muku **DevWorkspace Pro** - cikakken workspace don gina websites da mobile apps a cikin browser. Ba sai ka install komai ba - kawai buɗe shafi, ka fara coding."

---

## BABI 2: Ƙirƙiri Project (0:30 - 1:30)
**Screen:** Click ➕ a PROJECTS
**Ce:**
> "Ga projects ɗinmu. Danna plus don sabon project. A nan zaɓi template - Website, Firebase App, ko Dashboard. Zan zaɓi Website... shigar da suna... Create."
>
> "Ga shi! Ya ƙirƙira mana tsarin files gaba ɗaya - index.html, about, contact, assets folder, CSS, JavaScript - komai shirye."

---

## BABI 3: Editor da Emmet (1:30 - 2:30)
**Screen:** Buɗe index.html, rubuta `!` + Enter
**Ce:**
> "Wannan Monaco editor ne - kamar VS Code. Duba: syntax highlighting, line numbers."
>
> "A HTML, rubuta `!` sannan Enter - ga shi! HTML5 boilerplate cikakke ya zo."
>
> "Kuma Emmet: `ul>li.item*3` sannan Tab - list da items uku a ɗakiƙa. `nav>ul>li*5` - navbar!"
>
> "A CSS, kowane launi yana da square - click shi, color picker ya buɗe."

---

## BABI 4: Preview da Wayar Hannu (2:30 - 3:30)
**Screen:** Danna Run, zaɓi iPhone 14 a device select
**Ce:**
> "Danna Run - preview ya buɗe. Amma duba wannan - zan zaɓi iPhone 14... ga shi! Website ɗin yana cikin frame na wayar hannu."
>
> "Zan zaɓi Pixel 7... kowane size zaka iya testing. Auto-refresh yana aiki - kowane rubutu da nake yi, preview yana sabunta kai tsaye."

---

## BABI 5: Versions - Kamar Git (3:30 - 4:30)
**Screen:** VERSIONS tab, shigar da suna, Commit
**Ce:**
> "Wannan ɗaya daga cikin mafi kyawun fasali - VERSIONS. Kamar Git amma sauƙi."
>
> "Yi Commit kafin babban canji... yanzu idan na karya komai, danna Restore - komai ya koma yadda yake."
>
> "Danna Changes - duba! Wannan diff ne - abin da ya bambanta a kowane layi."

---

## BABI 6: AI Assistant (4:30 - 5:30)

**Ce:**
